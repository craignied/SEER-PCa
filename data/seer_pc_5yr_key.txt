Column 1: Age at diagnosis (years)
Column 2: Natural logarithm of PSA (ln[PSA in ng/mL])
Columns 3, 4: Gleason group (reference = 2-6: 7, 8-10)
Columns 5-8: AJCC T category (reference = T1: T0, T2, T3, T4)
Column 9: AJCC N category (reference = N0: N1)
Column 10: AJCC M category (reference = M0: M1)
Columns 11-13: Race/ethnicity (reference = White: Black, Hispanic, Other)
Columns 14-16: Marital status (reference = Married: Never married/domestic partner, Divorced/separated, Widowed)
Column 17: Previous cancer (0 = no, 1 = yes)
Column 18: Diagnosis year centered at 2004 (0 = 2004 through 5 = 2009)
Column 19: Area poverty (%)
Column 20: Area median household income (dollars)
Column 21: Area unemployment (%)
Column 22: Area population with less than high-school education (%)
Column 23: Prostate-cancer death within five years (0 = no, 1 = yes)
